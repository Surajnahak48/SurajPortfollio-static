About Me

I'm an AI/ML software engineer and a freelancer. My work runs from research and algorithm design through to the software that puts a model in front of the people who use it.

Day to day that means reading and testing ideas, designing and implementing algorithms, training and evaluating models for vision, language and prediction problems, and building the services around them.

I also build automation tools on top of that work, including tools for CAD and GIS teams that take repetitive drafting and mapping tasks off their hands.
